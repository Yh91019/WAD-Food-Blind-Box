DISCOVER SURPRISE MEALS,
REDUCE FOOD WASTE

Food Blind Box is a web-based project designed to promote sustainable food consumption through surprise meal boxes. Our platform allows users to purchase mystery food packages from restaurants and cafés at affordable prices.

Each blind box contains delicious surplus food that would otherwise go to waste. The goal of this project is to encourage people to reduce food waste while enjoying quality meals at lower prices. By connecting customers with food businesses, Blind Bite helps create a more sustainable community and supports environmental conservation through responsible food consumption.

Why Foodies Choose Blind Bite?

🍽️ Support Local Businesses

By purchasing Blind Bite boxes, you support local restaurants and cafés, helping them reduce food waste while growing their business.

🌱 Reduce Food Waste

Every blind box contains fresh, unsold food that is still perfectly safe to eat. Help reduce unnecessary food waste and support a greener planet.

💰 Save More

Enjoy delicious meals at discounted prices compared to their original value. Eat well while spending less.

🎁 Exciting Surprise Experience

Every Blind Bite is a mystery! Discover different meals, desserts, and beverages every time you order.

Our Story

Blind Bite transforms surplus food into exciting surprise meal boxes, helping reduce food waste while offering affordable and delicious meals.

Our Mission

To reduce food waste by connecting customers with affordable surprise meals and supporting local food businesses.

Our Vision

To create a future where good food is never wasted and sustainable dining becomes a way of life.
